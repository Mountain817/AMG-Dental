Our firm

An M&A firm focused on a single sector.

AMG Advisory advises laboratory owners, sector groups and investors on the purchase and sale of dental prosthesis laboratories in Spain.

How we work

Working in one sector means we know who buys in it, understand how value is built in a dental laboratory, and speak the same language as its owner, who is often also its most experienced technician.

  • Sector focus

    We work with dental prosthesis laboratories and with the groups and investors that acquire in the sector.

  • Preparation before contact

    We analyse the laboratory, its positioning and its potential buyers before any approach to third parties.

  • Discretion and direct contact

    Information is shared only with the owner's authorisation: neither the clinics nor the team learn of a process until the owner decides. Clients always work with the same people.

Structure

Part of AMG Advisory AS.

The M&A practice is part of AMG Advisory AS, a company registered in Norway under organisation number 938 271 062. AMG Advisory also advises on transactions in the fire-protection industry and provides digital-improvement and automation services to businesses. The M&A team works from Madrid.

The team brings prior experience in corporate and investment banking, financial analysis, and strategy and operations consulting.
