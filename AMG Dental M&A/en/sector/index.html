Sector

Dental prosthesis laboratories in Spain.

We focus on laboratories that design and make custom dental prostheses for clinics: a technical, regulated business built on trust between the laboratory and the dentist.

Focus

Custom-made dental prosthesis laboratories.

  • Fixed prosthetics: crowns and bridges in zirconia, lithium disilicate and metal-ceramic
  • Implant-supported prosthetics
  • Removable prosthetics: complete, partial and cast-frame dentures
  • Orthodontics, splints and appliances
  • CAD/CAM design, milling and 3D printing, including for other laboratories

We also understand the activities that often sit alongside these laboratories, such as milling centres, materials distribution and digital design services, and take them into account in every transaction.

Approach

How we assess each opportunity.

  • Activity and technology

    The mix of products and materials, how digital the laboratory is, and its production capacity.

  • Clinics and team

    The type of client clinics, how regularly they order, the technical team and the owner's day-to-day role.

  • Fit

    Which strategic or financial buyers have a clear reason to acquire the laboratory, and why.
